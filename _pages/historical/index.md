# Historical information and documents

The Bluffton University Nature Preserve had its beginning with a [study by the National Audubon Society](../assets/images//audubon_report.pdf) done for Bluffton College in 1966 with a plan for a "Rural Life Center".

Rather than creating a rural life center, the college decided to convert the college farm to a nature preserve, called the Swinging Bridge Nature Preserve. In the early years, the preserve had a director and did a lot of programming for area schools. 

[Pamphlet for the Nature Preserve in about 1970](../assets/images//preserve_pamphlet_c_1970.pdf)

[Map of the Preserve c. 1970](../assets/images//swinging_bridge_map.jpg)

Due to funding issues, the director position was eliminated and formal programming was reduced. However, the Preserve remained open.
