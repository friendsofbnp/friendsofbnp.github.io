### Description

Come learn about the invasive plant teasel and gain experience removing its seed heads. Gloves, hand pruners, and trash bags will be provided. 

No experience required.

All are welcome to attend.

Students will earn Arts & Lecture credit for one hour of work.

### Location

Meet at the south entrance of the Bluffton University Nature Preserve on Augsburger Rd.
