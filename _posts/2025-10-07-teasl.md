### Description

Teasel is an invasive species that competes with native species. Come help us remove it! Hand pruners, gloves, and bags will be provided.

No experience required.

All are welcome to attend.

Students will earn Arts & Lecture credit for one hour of work.

### Location

Meet at the south entrance of the Bluffton University Nature Preserve on Augsburger Rd.

### Date

Tuesday, 7 October 2024.

### Time

4:00-6:00 pm
