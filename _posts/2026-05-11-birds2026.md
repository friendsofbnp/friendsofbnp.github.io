### Description

Join Dr. Jacob Buchanan on a walk through the nature preserve as we listen and look for birds during their migration north. Expect to find a variety of showy spring migrants, including warblers, right here in our backyard. No experience needed and all ages are welcome. Binoculars will be available to borrow.

### Location

Meet at the Moyer Nature Center in the Preserve.
