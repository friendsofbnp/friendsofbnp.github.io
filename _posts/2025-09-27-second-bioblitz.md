A bioblitz is an opportunity for citizen scientists to help document the biodiversity of a natural area. Help us document as many living organisms as possible in our local preserves! 

<!--more-->

### Description

Help us document as many living organisms as possible at these important nature preserves!
Species ID skills are helpful but not required. Join us for a day in nature and contribute to a citizen-science program.

This program is for all ages. Participants will use Seek by iNaturalist on their mobile devices to identify and record species.

Hosted by Bluffton University and The University of Findlay

### Event schedule

9:00 - 10:00 AM: Moyer Nature Center, Bluffton University Nature Preserve, 10625 Augsburger Rd., Bluffton, OH

10:30 AM - 12:30 PM: Riek Center for Habitat Studies, 17311 TR 166, Mt. Blanchard, OH
