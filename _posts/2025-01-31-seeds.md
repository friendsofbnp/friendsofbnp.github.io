Come learn how to germinate native seeds. NOTE: offered on multiple dates.

### Description

Many native plant seeds require special conditions to break dormancy. Come learn about these requirements and start preparing some seeds for germination!

The event is open to all. Students in attendance will earn **Arts & Lecture credit**.

### Location

The Moyer Nature Center within the Bluffton University Nature Preserve on Augsburger Road.

### Dates and times

Tuesday, 11 February 2025 from 10:00 to 11:00 AM.

Wednesday, 12 February 2025 from 10:00 to 11:00 AM.

Saturday, 15 February from 10:00 to 11:00 AM.
