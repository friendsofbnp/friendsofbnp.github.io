Look for amazing spring migrants - no experience necessary. NOTE: offered on multiple dates. 

<!--more-->

### Description

No experience needed. Binoculars will be provided.

Everyone is encouraged to bring a fully-charged smartphone with the [Merlin app](https://merlin.allaboutbirds.org/) downloaded.

Everyone is welcome. Students will be eligible to receive Arts and Lecture credit for attending, up to a maximum of one time. This event will be held several times over a one-week timespan so that a broader audience can attend. 

### Location

The Moyer Nature Center within the Bluffton University Nature Preserve on Augsburger Road.

### Dates and times

Tuesday, 22 April 2025 at 9:00 AM.

Wednesday, 23 April 2025 at 9:00 AM. [eBird list of birds observed](https://ebird.org/checklist/S228493121)

Saturday, 26 April 2025 from 9:00 AM.
