### Description

Join Dr. Jacob Buchanan on a late spring nature walk. We expect to find a variety of flowering plants, insects, and resident summer birds. Come learn about the plants and wildlife in our nature preserve. 

No experience needed, and all ages are welcome. 

### Location

The Moyer Nature Center within the Bluffton University Nature Preserve on Augsburger Road.

### Dates and times

Thursday, 29 May 2025 from 6:00 to 7:00 PM.

Friday, 30 May 2025 from 6:00 to 7:00 PM.

Saturday, 31 May 2025 from 10:00 to 11:00 AM.
