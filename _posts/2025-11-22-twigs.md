Learn how to identify trees by their leafless twigs.

### Description

Join Dr. Jacob Buchanan and Dr. Bob Antibus on a nature walk as we learn to identify common trees by their bare, leafless twigs. 

Students will earn Arts & Lecture credit.

### Location

Meet at the Moyer Nature Center in the Bluffton University Nature Preserve.

### Date

Saturday, 22 November 2025.

### Time

noon-1:00 pm
