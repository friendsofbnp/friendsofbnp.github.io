Take a nature walk to see spring wildflowers! NOTE: offered on multiple dates. 

<!--more-->

### Description

During the nature walk, Dr. Buchanan will guide a group of participants along the trails surrounding the Moyer Nature Center. Participants will look for flowering trees and shrubs like maples, elms, aspens, and spicebush as well as early spring ephemeral wildflowers like Virginia spring beauty, harbinger-of-spring, and wild violets.

This event is open to all, including students, faculty, staff, and all other community members. Students in attendance will be eligible to receive Arts and Lecture credit for attending, up to a maximum of one time. This event will be held several times over a one-week timespan so that a broader audience can attend. 

### Location

The Moyer Nature Center within the Bluffton University Nature Preserve on Augsburger Road.

### Dates and times

Tuesday, 18 March 2025 from 4:00 to 5:00 PM.

Wednesday, 19 March 2025 from 4:00 to 5:00 PM.

Saturday, 22 March 2025 from 10:00 to 11:00 AM.

Sunday, 23 March from 4:00 to 5:00 PM.
