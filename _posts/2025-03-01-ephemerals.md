# Description

Join Dr. Jacob Buchanan on a nature walk to see early spring ephemerals like Virginia spring beauties, harbinger-of-spring, and wild violets. We may also see flowering trees and shrubs like maples, elms, aspens, and spicebush.

The event is open to all. Students in attendance will earn **Arts & Lecture credit**.

# Location

The Moyer Nature Center within the Bluffton University Nature Preserve on Augsburger Road.

# Dates and times

Tuesday, 18 March 2025 from 4:00 to 5:00 PM.

Wednesday, 19 March 2025 from 4:00 to 5:00 PM.

Saturday, 22 March 2025 from 10:00 to 11:00 AM.

Sunday, 23 March from 4:00 to 5:00 PM.
