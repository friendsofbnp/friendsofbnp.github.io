### Description

The Great Backyard Bird Count is a four-day long international event where participants go out to count and identify the birds that they find. We will spend 50 minutes birdwatching in the Bluffton University Nature Preserve as a group led by Dr. Jacob Buchanan. No experience needed. All ages and backgrounds are welcome. You may borrow a pair of binoculars. 

Bluffton University students will earn Arts & Lecture credit.

### Location

Meet at the Moyer Nature Center in the Bluffton University Nature Preserve.

### Dates

Friday, 13 Feb 2026
Saturday, 14 Feb 2026

### Time

11:00-11:50 am (both days)
